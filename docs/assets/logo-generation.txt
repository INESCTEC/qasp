Background removal, 3 November 2026:
The user requested the same logo without a background and explicitly approved
direct pixel-based removal. Generative extraction attempts were rejected because
they introduced edge artifacts; none of those attempts is used in these assets.
The original artwork was masked against its background colour. Logo interiors
were preserved byte-for-byte; a narrow antialiased edge band was unmatted against
the old background to avoid white/dark halos. Canvas, typography, geometry,
spacing and colours were not redesigned. Transparent node/letter holes and
appearance on white, dark and grey backgrounds were verified. Original opaque
assets remain available in Git history at cd2caed.
